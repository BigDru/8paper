Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

8paper is made by DEVELOPER_NAME ("we"). The short version: your notes stay on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and we do not collect personal data. The rest of this page explains the few cases where the app talks to anyone else.

Your notes

Notes are saved as SVG files on your device, either in the app's private storage or in a folder you choose. They are never sent to us. If you share or export a note, it goes only to the app or person you pick in Android's share screen.

Pictures you add to a note are copied into that note on your device.

Device backup

Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, depending on your device's backup settings. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; we cannot see it.

YouTube videos

If you paste a YouTube link into a note, the app connects to YouTube to show it:

  • it downloads the video's preview image from img.youtube.com;
  • for a playlist link, it asks youtube.com for the list of videos in it;
  • when you press play, the video plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com).

When this happens, YouTube receives the usual information any website gets, such as your IP address and device type, and the video you asked for. This is covered by Google's privacy policy. The app does not contact YouTube unless a note contains a YouTube link.

Purchases

Premium features are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details. The app asks Google Play on your device whether you own premium, and that is all it learns. See Google's privacy policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Permissions

The app asks only for internet access, used for the YouTube features and Google Play purchases described above.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we do not have any.

Children

8paper is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete your notes at any time in the app or by uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app starts collecting data (for example, an optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
